1

2
Five metric cards, one job. Each card click does exactly what the tab beneath it does. TikTok is missing here and from the "All" filter chips, although it has a tab and a modal entry.
3
A third route to the same place. Cards, tabs and the modal all call the same setTab() — three affordances, one function.
